Flight Recorder
For controllers with the Flight Recorder plugin. Every job is stamped and its lines numbered, so the controller remembers where it got to. If the connection drops it lifts the tool and stops the spindle, and when you reconnect FlexiSender offers to carry on from that line. Settings and the event log are in the FLIGHT RECORDER tab. While this is on FlexiSender doesn't reset the controller when it connects.
